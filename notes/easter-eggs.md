## Teardown (done)
Shake the shut phone hard 8 times in 5 s: the top cover pops off and its
parts spill across the cover screen to be put back.  fold3ds/teardown.lua,
art in tools/art/teardown_*.png (tools/make_teardown_sprites.py), toggle in
Settings > Shell & Controls.
